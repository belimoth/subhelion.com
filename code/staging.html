The Subhelion Trust is an Employee Ownership Trust (EOT) that owns and operates the Corporation in conjunction with the Foundation for the benefit of its authors and workers.

The Subhelion Foundation is a non-profit organization dedicated to creating a new future with cooperative business, structured community, and ethical technology. It accomplishes its mission by giving material and organizational support to the precarious and their champions.

The Subhelion Corporation is a for-profit production company for educational and entertainment media.
