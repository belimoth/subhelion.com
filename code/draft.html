Who are we for?

THE CHARITABLE To be truly charitable in this world you must put yourself at risk of disenfranchisement along with the meek you support. We treat allies as comrades-in-arms with standing. THE DISABLED, THE YOUNG, AND THE OLD The center of our society exists at its edge. We hold our various assemblies to practices that maximize meaningful participation from people that would otherwise be excluded. THE SUBALTERN This world is a place of cultural hegemony. We amplify marginalized voices and uplift indigineous and diaspora cultures to hold them in greater standing than our metropolitan elements. THE VIRTUOUS To be truly virtuous in this world you must put yourself at risk of disenfranchisement along with the weak you protect. Find meaningful work with us. THE WEARY Something new under the sun, for everyone else who wants to see the world grow.
